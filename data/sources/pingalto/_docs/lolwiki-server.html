Servers

From League of Legends Wiki, LoL Wiki
(Redirected from Server)
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Riot Games provides servers (or realms), each hosting the various aspects of League of Legends such as login sessions, matchmade queues and game sessions, the Store and others, and for various purposes.

The main, widely-available game is hosted in similar shards which are scattered throughout the world. Sharding alleviates the problems of high latency caused by long distances, avoids issues related to language barriers, and allows the delivering of localized content and patches more appropriately and timely to players, while maintaining identical game functions and a single game version.

Two public types of servers exist for League of Legends:

  1. Live, which is the stable version of the game and is sharded.
  2. PBE, which exists for the purposes of content reveals and general testing of tentative changes. It operates one patch cycle ahead and is hosted in North America.

Tencent China shards

[edit | edit source]

Servers in China are operated by Tencent Games, Riot Games' parent company. Tencent Games has established a number of Chinese (CN) shards for the People's Republic of China (PRC) since 2011. There are a total of 29 Live servers, 19 of which are hosted by China Telecom and 7 by China Unicom. Players can join servers hosted by a different Internet Service Provider but may encounter network problems due to irregular internet routing. Each of these servers may house up to 650,000 players. Some servers are dedicated to highly-skilled players, such as the Ionia server. Languages include Mandarin Chinese and Simplified Mandarin Chinese.

This server is accessible via the CERNET network that is used by some universities in China and offers lower latencies on average.

3. 峡谷之巅 (Super Zone/Super Server, literally translated as "Summit of the Valley/Rift")

This server is meant to scout potential players for LDL and LPL.[10] Entry requires Solo/Duo Diamond I and above in another server. Accounts automatically begin at level 30 and granted free Blue Essence meant for acquiring the necessary champion pool to unlock ranked play. Settlement date is the last day of the calendar month, new players will start counting the next month. On the settlement date, a player is removed if their recorded monthly games do not total at least 24 (reduced to 18 games in July), if they are the lowest ranked player, or the player has not upkept a rank of at least Diamond I.

Transferring to another server

[edit | edit source]

When creating a game account, the player must choose a server to play in. The server is usually the one that corresponds to their region/country and is pre-selected based on their location. Once created, the account belongs solely to the server in which was created and will only be able to play with other players in that server.

In order to use the same account information and content in another server and play alongside players of another server, a transfer can be purchased in the Riot Store. Players in the RU, BR, TR, VN and TW shards may not be able to transfer to some or most other servers. Yet others may not have any systems in place for transferability at all: KR game accounts require national ID credentials, while CN accounts are hosted by third-party companies; as such they are not listed here.

Server transfers, where available, always cost 2600 one-way. The table below shows the servers that members of each server can transfer into (green = available in the Store, black = not available).

Special clients

[edit | edit source]

Special game clients exist to facilitate tournaments and guests for testing, hosted separately from the main Live and PBE servers. They may undergo their own patch deployment and some are also sharded to collectively receive them.

Special clients may offer unique networking or lobby functionality to administrators and players, such as being able to select the server, creating offline (LAN) sessions and removing the spectator delay.

Esports Client

[edit | edit source]

Private servers exist for the purposes of esports tournaments and events and also uniquely permanently feature the Showdown mode. These cannot be found within the Riot API. Tencent-hosted realms are also not publicly available.

Accounts granted to players for the purposes of accessing esports clients' realms are issued in accordance with Riot's GCD (Global Contracts Database) and on a yearly basis otherwise by default.

Historical servers or clients

[edit | edit source]

SEA shards

[edit | edit source]

Before other servers opened to better split the playerbase, Garena offered the Singapore (SG) server as the default server for Southeast Asia players whose country or region was not part of other servers. Upon signing up or logging in to Garena, those players would enter the SG server.

Below is a table listing the first set of Southeast Asia servers (under Garena hosting).

The Indonesia (ID) server was shut down on May 16th, 2019, 00:01 WIB (GMT+7).[11] The player base was asked to transfer to the now consolidated "Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia (SG)" server. Transfers could still take place for some time after server closure (however, only those who have transferred their accounts before closure would receive the incentive rewards offered for transferring).

On November 26th, 2024 however, Riot announced the merging servers of Thailand (TH2) and the Philippines (PH2) into the Singapore, Malaysia, & Indonesia (SG2) server, citing improved matchmaking and latency.[12] The TH2 and PH2 servers were permanently shut down at the end of the patch V14.24 cycle (9 January 2025, 03:00:00 local time). The SG2 server was renamed to "Southeast Asia" (SEA) and now hosts all their regions combined.[13]

Garena hosting

[edit | edit source]

Main Article: Garena

Garena is a consumer Internet platform provider based in Asia that used to handle publishing League of Legends (ergo Teamfight Tactics PC too) in the region.

Each server had its own headquarters and its own pricing for champions, skins, bundles etc. Server transfer was not available to and from this region; players were required to be part of the Garena cyberspace exclusively.

On January 5th, 2023, Riot Games terminated their contract with Garena. This resulted in Riot assuming all publishing rights for both League of Legends and Teamfight Tactics on all respective countries. Riot in turn had to partner with VNG Corporation and Taiwan Mobile for players in the regions of Vietnam (VN) and Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau (TW) due to certain state laws.[14] Riot Games initially kept an identical regional split. They would also serve the same regions that the previous shards did.

Doran's Workshop

[edit | edit source]

Doran's Workshop was a private server where tentative and experimental changes were tested and iterated on.[15] It was originally called the Private Test Realm (PTR) This server was only available to Riot Games Inc. employees and hand-selected members of the community.

The server and forums were permanently closed on July 18, 2012 following a leak of patch notes for an experimental champion remake.

Test Realm

[edit | edit source]

The Test Realm was a client that accompanied the only live game server at the time (meant for North America) and was used by Riot developers and beta testers for feedback on proposed content or bugs. It existed roughly during the 2009 to 2010 period (Alpha, Beta and release Season One) and was the first place where new champions, items, maps, and other changes were revealed before launch.

Very few and involved players from the League of Legends community had access to the server, and rarely, Riot would host giveaways or send out invitations to members of the broader community. The Test Realm was deprecated after new internal testing systems and teams were formed as well as with the release of the Public Beta Environment, where content changes and additions would be revealed to the general public at once.

Below is a compilation of official posts related to the Test Realm. General Rules[16]

  • If you are being unhelpful, spamming, consistently leaving matchmade games, or just being disruptive in general, you will be banned without warning.
  • Test Realm access is a privilege, not a right and can be revoked at any time for any reason.
  • Please keep topics organized [in the forums] and do not open new topics when there is already an existing active thread.

Confidentiality Quoting Pendragon's post in the forums. "[...] That being said - we're seeing more and more instances of test-realm content being posted to the general section of this site, and to other sites.

I want to reinforce that test-realm content is considered to be confidential outside of this forum section, that means:

  • You should not be taking or releasing screenshots of unreleased content
  • You should not be releasing guides (including videos) for unreleased characters
  • You should not be discussing ANYTHING on the test realm in-game, on other sites, or outside of this forum section"

"Whether [the content]'s available or not - we require at the moment that [the Test Realm Forum is] the only source for it"
