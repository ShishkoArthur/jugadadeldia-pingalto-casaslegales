    Xbox Cloud Gaming Now Available in Argentina and New Zealand

    Array
    June 9, 2022 · 2 min read

    It remains our mission at Xbox to enable the gaming community to play the games they want, with the people they want, on the devices they already own. It’s about providing you more ways to connect and play by removing barriers.

    There are many ways we’re doing this at Xbox, one of which is through Xbox Cloud Gaming – this makes it possible to play console games on phones, tablets, PCs, and more devices all through the cloud. Launched in 2020, we’ve continued to iterate our cloud gaming experience with feedback from the community, have added more great content, and have expanded to more places. Today, I’m proud to announce we’re continuing to expand by bringing Xbox Cloud Gaming to New Zealand and Argentina.

    We’ve continuously heard from gamers in Argentina and New Zealand that accessing Xbox Cloud Gaming is a priority and we’re thrilled to make it a reality. Xbox Cloud Gaming (Beta) begins rolling out later today so you can soon play Halo Infinite, Forza Horizon 5, and Tunic directly from the cloud with an Xbox Game Pass Ultimate membership on iOS devices, Android phones and tablets, Windows PCs, Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, and, as announced today, in the next few weeks you’ll be able to play on smart TVs with our launch partner Samsung.

    Now New Zealand and Argentina fans can join the party and play Fortnite through Xbox Cloud Gaming, no subscription required. All you need to do is go to xbox.com/play, sign in with your Xbox account, and start playing. And if you are an Xbox Game Pass Ultimate member, you can continue your game, start something completely new, or discover your next favorite title among hundreds of cloud-playable games in the Game Pass catalog. For those of you on the go, there are more than 100 games featuring touch controls including Fortnite, so you can jump right in without the need for a controller. All available to play from the cloud across the devices you already own, without the need to download or use your device storage

    It’s an exciting day at Xbox, as we shared more about what’s next for gaming. And we’re so thrilled that part of that journey is bringing Xbox Cloud Gaming to gamers in Argentina and New Zealand. We look forward to having you play and share your experience.
